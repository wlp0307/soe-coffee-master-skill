2. 课程说明与更新 Course Description and Updates
说明 Description
感官品鉴中级课程内容以初级课程所介绍的概念与技能为基础，相当适合已具有杯测经验、有意开发感官技能及增进感官科学知识的咖啡同好。本课程涵盖众
多主题，包括：味道与香气的生理学知识、感官测试法及其应用、杯测会流程、咖啡属性的多样性、使用咖啡风味轮以及 WCR 感官辞典，与如何建置感官课程
或评估小组。
The Sensory Skills Intermediate course builds on the concepts and skills introduced in the Sensory Skills Foundation course. It is ideal for someone who has cupping
experience and wants to develop their sensory skills and knowledge of sensory science. This course covers a wide range of topics: the physiology of taste and aroma, the
types of sensory test methods and their applications, the operation of a cupping session, the diversity of coffee attributes, the use of the Coffee Taster’s Flavor Wheel and
WCR Sensory Lexicon, and the implementation of a sensory program or panel.
旧版课程更新 Curriculum Updates from Previous Version
旧版课程指南为 2.0 版，本课程指南为 3.0 版。
The previous Guidebook is referred to as Version 2.0. This Guidebook is referred to as Version 3.0.
在 3.0 版中，目标、内容、活动均已依咖啡价值评估的标准与工具予以调整。尽管整体目标维持不变，本课程如今纳入了更多感官科学的重要概念，并确保学
员使用 CVA 标准规范以及 SCA 描述性与情感性评估表体验杯测。部分章节已重新排序或改写，以充分诠释并易于理解，给予AST 的注释也已扩充。
In Version 3.0, the objectives, content, and activities have been adjusted to reflect Coffee Value Assessment protocols and tools. While the overarching goals of the
module remain the same, the course now includes more key concepts from sensory science and ensures that learners experience cupping using the CVA Protocol and the
SCA Descriptive and Affective Forms. Some sections have been reordered or reworded to improve clarity. AST Notes have been expanded.
所有文件（课程教材）均可至使用者入口网站下载（My AST Portal > My AST Downloads > Course Materials）。
All documents are available in the user portal (My AST Portal > My AST Downloads > Course Materials).
