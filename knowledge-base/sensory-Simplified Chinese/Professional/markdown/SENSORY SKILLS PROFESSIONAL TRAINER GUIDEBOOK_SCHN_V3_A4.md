2. 课程说明与更新 Course Description and Updates
说明 Description
感官品鉴专业级课程以中级课程所介绍的概念和技能为基础，旨在协助学员于从事咖啡和相关产品的感官评估时善尽管理职能。在本交互式课程中，
学员除可增进自身对咖啡业界标准与感官科学最佳实务的认识外，还能探索咖啡业进行感官评估的情况、鉴别及评估精品咖啡生豆的质量，并示范如
何精准确测量及描述咖啡饮品的特点。参与者将学习如何生成可重复且系统性的咖啡感官测量数据，并解读相关结果。课程结束后将透过笔试测验学
员的专业课程知识，并根据课程期间进行的各项实作活动，以实作方式测验上述技能。
The Sensory Skills Professional course builds on the concepts and skills introduced in the Sensory Skills Intermediate course and prepares the learner for
managerial job functions in the sensory assessment of coffee and related products. In this interactive course, learners gain alignment with coffee industry
standards and sensory science best practices, explore the implementation of sensory assessment in a coffee business, identify and evaluate qualities in specialty
green coffee, and demonstrate how to accurately measure and describe coffee beverage characteristics. Participants will learn how to generate repeatable and
methodical sensory measurements of coffee, along with interpretation of those results. A written exam tests professional course knowledge while a practical
exam tests the skills described above based on different working activities carried out during the course.
旧版课程更新 Curriculum Updates from Previous Version
旧版课程指南为 2.0 版，本课程指南为 3.0 版。
The previous Guidebook is referred to as Version 2.0. This Guidebook is referred to as Version 3.0.
在 3.0 版中，目标、内容、活动均已依咖啡价值评估（CVA）的标准与工具予以调整。尽管整体目标维持不变，本课程如今纳入了更多感官科学的重要
概念，并透过 CVA 标准以及SCA 描述性和情感性评估表帮助学习。部分章节已重新排序或改写，以充分诠释并易于理解，给予 AST 的注释也已扩充。
In Version 3.0, the objectives, content, and activities have been adjusted to reflect Coffee Value Assessment protocols and tools. While the overarching goals of
the module remain the same, the course now includes the CVA Protocol and the SCA Descriptive and Affective Forms, as well as more key concepts from sensory
science. Some sections have been reordered or reworded to improve clarity. AST Notes have been expanded.
更新后的课程教材以及实作测验试卷，请至 AST Portal > My AST Downloads下载。
Curricula and exam updates are available in the AST Portal under My Downloads.

each cup, which is ground and brewed independently.

Descriptive Test A descriptive test is a quantitative sensory test that seeks to profile a product on all its perceived sensory attributes.
差别测试 差别（区别性）测试是一种感官测试，用于辨别样本之间是否存在差异（或没有差异）。
Difference Test A difference (discriminative) test is a sensory test that identifies whether there is a difference (or not) between samples.

Mouthfeel 感）。

Olfactory Relating to the sense of smell. The human olfactory system consists of the nose, the nasal cavities, and the “olfactory bulb” in the brain, which
