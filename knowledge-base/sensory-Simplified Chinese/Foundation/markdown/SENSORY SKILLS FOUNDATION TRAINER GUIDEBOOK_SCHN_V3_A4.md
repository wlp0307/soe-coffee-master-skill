2. 课程说明与更新 Course Description and Updates
说明 Description
感官品鉴初级课程以实作和互动方式，探索感官评估时不可或缺的要素。本课程探讨我们感知品味的方式，以及在评估咖啡的特性时如何运用此知
识，学员将获得实际辨识各种感官属性的经验，以及使用 SCA 描述性与情感性两种杯测评估方式。
The Sensory Skills Foundation course explores the essentials of sensory assessment in a practical and interactive manner. It investigates the way we perceive
what we taste and how to apply this knowledge when evaluating a coffee’s characteristics. Learners will gain practical experience identifying a variety of sensory
attributes and cupping coffees using both the SCA Descriptive and Affective Assessments.
旧版课程指南更新 Guidebook Updates from Previous Version
旧版课程指南为 2.0 版，本课程指南为 3.0 版。
The previous Guidebook is referred to as Version 2.0. This Guidebook is referred to as Version 3.0.
在 3.0 版中，目标、内容、活动均已依咖啡价值评估（CVA）的标准规范和用具予以调整。尽管整体目标维持不变，本课程如今纳入了更多感官科学的
关键概念，并确保学员使用 CVA 标准规范以及 SCA 描述性与情感性表格体验杯测。部分章节已重新排序或改写，以充分诠释并易于理解，给予AST 的
注释也已扩充。
In Version 3.0, the objectives, content, and activities have been adjusted to reflect Coffee Value Assessment protocols and tools. While the overarching goals of
the module remain the same, the course now includes more key concepts from sensory science and ensures that learners experience cupping using the CVA
Protocol and the SCA Descriptive and Affective Forms. Some sections have been reordered or reworded to improve clarity. AST Notes have been expanded.
更新后的课程教材以及实作测验试卷，请至 AST Portal > My AST Downloads下载。
Curricula and exam updates are available in the AST Portal under My Downloads.

Specialty coffee is a coffee or coffee experience recognized for its distinctive attributes, and because of these attributes, has significant extra value in

咖啡豆秤重后放在独立杯中。
Weigh coffee in individual cups as whole beans.
研磨样本，在每个样本间清洁磨豆机。
Grind samples, purging grinder in between samples.

Evaluate dry fragrance within 15 minutes of grinding.
注入符合 SCA 标准、温度介于摄氏 90 至 96 度之间（华氏 195 至 205 度）的水。

浸泡 3 至 5 分钟；保持每次冲泡的一致性。

破渣（不撒出咖啡渣）。搅拌三次（评估湿香）。

捞除表面的泡泡与浮渣。
Skim the surface bubbles and surface grounds.
浸泡。放凉到摄氏 70 度（大约华氏 160 度)，约 8 至 10 分钟。
Steep. Let cool to 70°C (about 160°F), about 8-10 minutes.
吸入香气并品尝；漱口、吐掉、记录，然后重复。
Aspirate and taste; rinse, spit, document, and repeat.
