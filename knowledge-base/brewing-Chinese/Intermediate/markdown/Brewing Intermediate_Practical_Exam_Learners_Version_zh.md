2. 冲煮测试 Brewing Test
完成以下任务的时间为 40 分钟。You have 40 minutes to complete the following Parts.
请选择培训中所使用的任意 2 种冲煮设备（自动咖啡机除外）。Please choose any 2 brewing devices that are
available at this campus (except an automatic brewer).
请分别使用每种设备准备一份冲煮，溶出物浓度最低为 1.15%。Please prepare one brew from each device that
achieves a minimum solubles concentration of 1.15%.
如果您选择了过滤式冲煮，则该冲煮成品的萃取率需为18%-22%。If you choose a drip filter brewer, the brew
must also achieve a solubles yield of 18% to 22% (a successful brew).
如果您选择了替代的冲煮器材，则必须参照使用AST规定的水粉比，而且沖煮成品的最低可溶物濃度
必須達到1.15%（所謂成功的沖煮）。If you choose an alternative device, you must use a coffee to water ratio
within the range described by the AST, and the brew must achieve a minimum solubles concentration of 1.15% (a successful
brew).
您可以分别使用每种设备准备多次冲煮，但仅可提交其中一份冲煮进行评分。Multiple brews may be
prepared with each device, but only one brew from each device may be submitted for scoring.
请为在此部分准备的每一份冲煮分别填写一份工作表（包括折射仪分析） 提交相应的工作表后，方可
获得成功冲煮的分数。You are required to complete a worksheet (including refractometer analysis) for each brew you
prepare in this section. A successful brew will not receive points unless you submit a corresponding worksheet.
如果您使用第一台设备成功冲煮，并提交了相应的工作表，则可获得 8 分。 如果您使用此设备成功冲
煮，并正确填写工作表，则可额外获得 2 分。If you submit a successful brew from the first device, and a
corresponding worksheet, you will be awarded 8 points. If your brew from this device is successful, and your worksheet is
completed correctly, you will be awarded an additional 2 points.
如果您使用第二台设备成功冲煮，并提交了相应的工作表，则可获得 8 分。 如果您使用此设备成功冲
煮，并正确填写工作表，则可额外获得 2 分。If you submit a successful brew from the second device, and a
corresponding worksheet, you will be awarded 8 points. If your brew from this device is successful, and your worksheet is
completed correctly, you will be awarded an additional 2 points.

3. 理想杯故障排除测试 Ideal Cup Troubleshooting Test
完成以下任务的时间为 30 分钟。You have 30 minutes to complete the following tasks.
在任务 3.1、3.2, 和 3.3 中，您不可与其他学生商量。In Part 3.1, 3.2, and 3.3 you may not consult with other
students.
如有必要，则计时器将在您完成任务 3.3 之后到开始任务 3.4 之前的这段时间内停止。If necessary, the
timer will be stopped between the completion of Part 3.3 and the beginning of Part 3.4.
如发现设备异常，请告知评估人，其可酌情停止计时。If you believe any of the equipment is not functioning
correctly, please inform the assessor who may stop the time if appropriate.
如有必要，评估人还可重启测试，并酌情重置计时器。If necessary, a task may be restarted, and the timer reset
appropriately.
任务 3.1
评估人使用自动过滤咖啡机冲煮了咖啡，因为是有意为之，所以咖啡萃取率低于 18%（萃取不足）或
高于 22%（过度萃取）。The AST has brewed a coffee with an automatic filter brewer, and intentionally brewed the
coffee so that it has achieved either a solubles yield below 18% (under extracted), or a solubles yield above 22% (over
extracted).
您必须品尝并说明冲煮咖啡属于萃取不足还是过度萃取。You must taste the coffee and indicate whether you
think the brew is under extracted, or over extracted.
如果您的味道分析正确，则可获得 4 分。If your taste analysis is correct, you will be awarded 4 points.
任务 3.2
评估人会告诉您用于准备此次冲煮的咖啡粉量和用水量。 您必须据此计算此次冲煮的粉水比，并使用
折射仪测量冲煮样品，判断溶出物浓度。The AST will then tell you the ground coffee and water doses used to
prepare the brew. You must then calculate the coffee to water ratio used for the brew, and then measure a sample of the brew
with a refractometer to determine the solubles concentration.
必须遵循正确的样品测量方案。Correct sample measuring protocols must be followed.
使用该粉水比和溶出物浓度，并结合冲煮品管表，判断萃取率。You must then use the coffee to water ratio and
the solubles concentration to determine the solubles yield with the brewing control chart.
如果您的计算正确，并正确使用折射仪和冲煮品管表，则可获得 4 分。If your calculations and use of the
refractometer and brewing control chart are correct, you will be awarded 4 points.
任务 3.3
您必须检查自动过滤咖啡机和研磨机，并诊断造成萃取不足或过度萃取的原因。如果您的诊断正确，则
可获得 4 分。You must then examine the automatic filter brewer and grinder and diagnose the cause of the brew being
under or over extracted. If your diagnosis is correct, you will be awarded 4 points.
必须描述修正冲煮的解决方案。Then you must describe a solution to correct the brew.
如果您的解决方案正确，则可获得 4 分。If your solution is correct you will be awarded 4 points.
如果您的诊断和解决方案均正确，则可进入任务 3.4。If your diagnosis and solution are correct, you may proceed
to Part 3.4.
否则不得进入任务 3.4。If your diagnosis and solution are not correct, you may not proceed to Part 3.4.
任务 3.4
现在您必须使用您的解决方案，在同一台自动过滤咖啡机上准备冲煮，并且溶出物浓度为 1.15% -
1.45%，萃取率为 18% - 22%。 必须使用与评估人所用相同的粉水比。You must now use your solution to
prepare a brew in the same automatic filter brewer, and achieve a solubles concentration of 1.15% to 1.45%, and a solubles
yield of 18% to 22%. You must use the same coffee to water ratio as was used by the AST.
您可以准备多次冲煮来获得目标冲煮，但是目标冲煮必须在指定时间内完成。You may prepare multiple
brews to achieve the target brew, but the target brew must be completed within the allocated time.
如果您在指定时间内成功冲煮，则可获得 4 分。If you prepare a successful brew within the allocated time, you will
be awarded 4 points.
