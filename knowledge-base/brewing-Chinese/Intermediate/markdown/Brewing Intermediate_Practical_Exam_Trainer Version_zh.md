概述 Overview
实践考试的目的在于测试学生的以下能力：The intention of the practical exam is to test the student’s ability to:
• 确定冲煮咖啡的浓度和萃取率差异；Identify strength and extraction differences in brewed coffee;
• 使用不同设备准备冲煮，萃取率为 18% - 22%，溶出物浓度至少为 1.15%，以及；Prepare brews
from different devices, each at 18% - 22% solubles yield, and each at a minimum of 1.15% solubles concentration, and;
• 修正自动过滤咖啡机冲煮失败的咖啡配方。Diagnose how to correct the recipe for a poorly brewed coffee from
an Automatic Filter Brewer.
学生必须获得 70% 以上分数才能通过实践考试。The student must score at least 70% to pass the practical exam.
学生必须正确识别第 1a 部分中的至少一种冲煮，还要正确识别第 1b 部分中的至少一种冲煮，才能获
得通过实践考试所需的最低总分。. Students must correctly identify at least 1 brew in Part 1a, and also correctly
identify at least 1 brew in Part 1b to achieve the minimum overall points necessary to pass this practical exam.
考试用咖啡 Coffee for Exams
第 1a 和 1b 部分所用咖啡必须是课堂活动和实践中使用过的咖啡，学生应熟悉其参考冲煮。The coffee
used in parts 1a and 1b must be a coffee that has been used for activities and practice during the class, and the student should
be familiar with reference brews of this coffee.
第 2 和 3 部分所用咖啡必须是课堂活动或实践中未曾使用过的咖啡。The coffee used in parts 2 and 3 must be
a coffee that has not been used for activities or practice during the class.
第 1 部分 - 味道测试 Part 1 - Taste tests
用于第 1a 和 1b 部分冲煮的咖啡在隔热玻璃瓶中存放不应超过 1 小时，装杯之后，不应冷却至室温。
The coffees brewed for Parts 1a and 1b should be held in insulated flasks for no longer than 1 hour, and once served, should
not be allowed to cool to room temperature.

第 3 部分 - 理想杯故障排除 Part 3 - Ideal Cup Troubleshooting
在考试的第 3 部分（理想杯故障排除）中，AST 必须准备一份不在“理想”范围内的自动过滤咖啡机
冲煮的咖啡，使用 1:15 - 1:19 之间的粉水比，并要求学生在仅更改一个要素的前提下修正冲煮。In Part
3 of the exam (Ideal Cup Troubleshooting), the AST must prepare an Automatic Filter brew that is not in the “Ideal” range, using
a coffee to water ratio between 1:15 - 1:19, and requires the student to make a change to only one element to correct the brew.
AST 必须仅通过以下要素之一，冲煮出“萃取不足”或“过度萃取”的咖啡：The AST must use only one
of the following elements to produce either an under or over extracted brew:
• 研磨设置不当 Incorrect grind setting
• 水温不当 Incorrect water temperature
• 浸润时间（预浸润）不当 Incorrect wetting time (pre-wet)
• 给水时间不当 Incorrect water delivery time
AST 必须填写一份冲煮工作表，以便记录研磨设置、水温、浸润时间和给水时间，以供参考。The AST
must complete a worksheet for the brew so that the grind setting, water temperature, wetting time, and water delivery time are
recorded for reference.
建议 AST 在此故障排除测试中使用能产生明显“萃取不足”或“过度萃取”特征的咖啡，AST 意在使
此咖啡的萃取率比其在均衡冲煮情况下的萃取率至少高或低 2%，并在“SCA 理想”范围之外。It is
recommended that the AST uses a coffee in this troubleshooting test that is capable of producing clearly over or under
extracted characteristics, and the AST aims for a solubles yield that is at least 2% more or less than a well-balanced brew of
this coffee, as well as being outside the “SCA Ideal” range.
学生要在 30 分钟内完成以下任务：The student has 30 minutes to complete the following:
• 味道分析 Taste analysis
• 粉水比计算和折射仪分析 Coffee to water ratio calculation and refractometer analysis
• 诊断 Diagnosis
• 填写解决方案 Write a solution
• 准备修正后的冲煮。Prepare a corrected brew.
建议（但不作要求）学生使用工作表填写修正后的冲煮。It is recommended, but not required, that students use
worksheets to complete the corrected brew.
AST 应在每位学生参加此部分考试之前校准折射仪，学生在使用折射仪时必须使用正确的样品测量方
案。The AST should calibrate the refractometer prior to each student attempting this part of the exam, and students must use

学生必须使用与 AST 所用相似或相同的咖啡机准备修正后的冲煮。The student must prepare the corrected
brew in the same or identical brewer used by the AST.
由 AST 对学生的冲煮咖啡进行测量，判断其是否符合“SCA 理想”范围。The student’s brew is measured
by the AST to determine if it is within the “SCA Ideal” range.
可在指定时间内准备多次冲煮。Multiple brews may be prepared within the allocated time.
如果自动过滤冲煮设备数量有限，学生可在任务 3.4 中结对准备修正后的冲煮。If automatic filter brewing
equipment is limited, students may work in pairs to prepare the corrected brew in Part 3.4.
