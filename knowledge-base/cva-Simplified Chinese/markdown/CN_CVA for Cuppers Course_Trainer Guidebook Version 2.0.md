1. 一般信息
General Information
课程长度：最少 16 小时Course Length: Minimum 16 Hours
先修课程：无。本课程乃针对使用 2004 年 SCA 杯测标准的现任杯测师，包括但不限于：专
业杯测师、现任或前咖啡质量鉴定师（Q Arabica Graders），以及每月至少使用一次 SCA
杯测系统或过去数年内频繁使用该系统者。Prerequisite(s): None. This course is for current cuppers who
utilize the 2004 SCA cupping protocol, including but not limited to: Professional cuppers, Q Arabica Graders or
former Q Arabica Graders, people who use the SCA cupping system at least once a month or have used it
intensively at some point in the past few years.
本课程可采面授或在线方式进行。如果讲师选择以在线方式进行授课，则必须（1）使用支持
实时授课的视频会议平台或工具，以及（2）与学员协调，确保他们在进行活动时获得适当材
料与指导。详情请见附录 D：进行交互式在线课程。This course can be delivered in person or online. If
the trainer chooses to deliver the course online, they must (1) utilize a video conferencing platform or tool that allows
for live instruction, and (2) coordinate with learners to ensure they have the appropriate supplies and guidance for
conducting activities. See Appendix D: Facilitating an Interactive Online Course for more details.
笔试总题数：30 题（每题一分。）Total Number of Questions on Written Exam: 30 (Each question is worth
one point.)
笔试时间总长：32 分钟Total Time Allowed for Written Exam: 32 Minutes
及格分数（笔试）：80% Passing Score (Written Exam): 80%
学员必须通过笔试以及实作测验，始可获颁课程证书。实作测验包含香气类以及描述性评估
（包括强度量表与 CATA 表）、情感性评估以及外在评估。关于各项实作测验的补充信息，请
见附录 C：讲师评分表与评分标准。Learners must pass the written exam and each practical exam
component to receive the course certificate. The practical exam components include a aroma categorization
component, a descriptive assessment component (including both intensity and CATA elements), an affective
assessment component, and an extrinsic assessment component. For additional details on grading each practical
exam component, see Appendix C: Trainer Scoresheet and Grading Criteria.
每项测验均可补考一次。实作测验的考试次数将备注在课程大纲中（面授课程）及课程模块说
明中（在线课程）。讲师应留存包含学员评估表副本在内的相关资料，以便必要时向学员提供
反馈。Learners are offered two attempts to pass each exam component. Practical exam attempts are noted in
the course agenda (for in-person courses) and in the module breakdown (for online courses). Trainers should keep
records, including copies of learners’ assessment forms, for a reasonable amount of time and be able to provide
feedback as needed to learners.
由于杯测应用程序可为用户提供额外辅助，并防止他们犯下某些错误，因此在实作测验部分中
不允许使用杯测应用程序。然而，讲师仍应鼓励学员在课程结束后使用 CVA 杯测应用程序。
Because cupping applications provide additional assistance to users and prevent them from making certain
mistakes, the use of cupping applications for practical exam components is not permitted. However, trainers should
still encourage learners to try CVA-based cupping applications after completing the course.

2. 课程说明与更新
Course Description and Updates
杯测师咖啡价值评估课程旨在针对咖啡杯测师提供专业的更新内容。在本课程中，学员将探索
如何在杯测实作中使用并整合 SCA 咖啡价值评估（CVA）的各项工具。本课程包含各种实作
活动，包括若干杯测、课程讲授以及分组讨论。The Coffee Value Assessment for Cuppers Course is a
professional update for coffee cuppers. During the course, learners will explore how to use and integrate the SCA
Coffee Value Assessment (CVA) tools into their cupping practice. The course includes a variety of hands-on
activities, including several cuppings, as well as lecture and group discussions.
学员在完成杯测师 CVA 课程后，将能够：After completing the CVA Course for Cuppers, learners will be
able to:
• 识别并使用 CVA 感官参照物。After completing the CVA for Cuppers Course, learners will be able to:
• 针对 CVA 所使用的主要感官评估工具解释其功能与背景，例如：15 分强度量表、CATA
表以及 9 分情感性量表。Explain the function and background of key sensory assessment tools used in
CVA—15-point intensity scales, CATA lists, and nine-point affective scales.
• 使用 CVA 描述性、情感性以及外在等评估表描述并评估咖啡。Use the CVA Descriptive,
Affective, and Extrinsic Forms to describe and evaluate coffee.
• 说明并使用新的 CVA 系统，包括描述性、情感性以及外在评估工具。Explain and use the new
CVA system, including the descriptive, affective, and extrinsic assessment tools.

themselves. Write each question or concern on a board, large notepad, etc. so that all learners can see them.

this at the end of Day 2 and respond to any questions or concerns that remain.
• 在学员自我介绍后，请学员分享他们对于 2004 年杯测方法最喜欢的一点，以及最不喜
欢的一点。在授课期间，寻找机会将CVA相关主题与这些学员的评论连结起来。Ask
each learner to share one thing they liked and one thing they didn’t like about the 2004 cupping methodology
after they introduce themselves. Look for opportunities throughout the course to connect CVA topics back to
these comments.

1.2 介绍 CVA
Introduction to CVA
学习目标Learning Objectives:
1. 说明 CVA 系统背后的核心概念。Explain the core concepts behind the CVA system.
说明 CVA 的背景及其如何支持咖啡价值链中不同部分。学员可能会提出许多问题，请预留充
足的时间来回答问题。重点强调以下几点：Explain the background of the CVA and how it can support

1.3 了解 15 分强度量表
Understanding the 15-Point Intensity Scale
学习目标Learning Objectives:
1. 说明 15 分强度量表的功能与背景。Explain the function and background of the 15-point intensity
scale.
咖啡最受重视的属性之一就是感官属性，为了衡量感官属性，我们需要使用来自感官科学领
域的技术。咖啡价值评估中的「杯测」部分就是一系列感官测试，我们使用这些测试来评估
与咖啡饮用体验相关的感官属性，包括味道、气味、风味与口感。Among the most valued
attributes a coffee can have are its sensory attributes. To measure sensory attributes, we need to use techniques
that come to us from the discipline of sensory science. The “cupping” portion of the Coffee Value Assessment is a
set of sensory tests. We use these to assess the sensory attributes we associate with drinking coffee, including
tastes, smells, flavors, and textures.
在感官科学中，测量感官属性的方法主要有三种，其各自以不同方式评估感官属性：In
sensory science, there are three distinct kinds of sensory tests, which can measure sensory attributes in different
ways:
• 第一种是「区别性」测试：简单而言，这种测试用于评估辨别两个或多个样本差异的能
力。The first is a “discriminative” test: simply, the ability to tell the difference—to discriminate—between
two or more samples.
• 第二种是「描述性」测试：此方法使用标准化的词汇来描述 感官体验。The second kind of
test is the “descriptive” test in which the user describes the sensory experience using a standardized
vocabulary.
• 第三种是「情感性」测试：主要用于评估样本的质量或喜好度。The third kind of test is the
“affective” test, which measures quality or liking.
这是在任何精品咖啡公司杯测室经常会听到的一段话：「我品尝了三款咖啡，我最喜欢第三
款，它尝起来有花香和甜味，是最棒的一款咖啡！」如您所见，这一段话就包含了三种类型
的声明：品尝者能够辨别不同咖啡的区别性声明、咖啡带有花香和甜味的描述性声明，以及
两个情感性声明（关于喜好及质量）。Here is a statement that would be very familiar in the cupping
room of any specialty coffee company: “I tasted three coffees. I liked the third one. It tasted floral and sweet—it
was the best!” As you can see, this single statement contains all three types of claims: the discriminative claim
that the taster can distinguish between coffees, the descriptive claim that the coffee was floral and sweet, and
two affective claims (one about liking and the other about quality.)
由于我们的语言中涵盖了这些类别，它们也往往被包含在杯测标准规范中。在「旧版」
2004 年 SCA 杯测表中 [播放 2004 年杯测表的蓝色与灰色区块]：浅灰色区块属于情感性
评项，蓝色区块是描述性评项，深灰色区块则是区别性评项。在感官科学中，将这些类别混
合在一起评估被认为是不好的做法，因为这些本质上是不同的咖啡评估方式。将描述性与情
感性元素分开，我们能更准确、更真实地评估咖啡。Because we include all these categories in our
language, we have often included them all in our cupping protocols. See here on the “old” 2004 SCA form [Show
Blue and Gray Section of 2004 Form]: the light gray sections are affective, the blue sections are descriptive, and
the dark gray section is discriminative. This is considered bad practice in sensory science, since these are
fundamentally different ways of evaluating coffee. By separating descriptive and affective elements of the
cupping form, we can be much more accurate and truthful when evaluating a coffee.

• 对质量印象意见分歧乃正常现象，关于质量印象没有绝对的「对」或「错」。Diverging

咖啡感官属性的情感性评估是我们如何评价咖啡的一个重要部分，尤其是在精品咖啡领域。

understanding the difference between the two is a big part of appraising a coffee’s value.

种刺激或多重刺激评估其接受度、喜好、偏好或情感，都属于情感性评估。Whereas
descriptive assessment answers the question “What does it taste like?” affective assessment answers a different
category of questions, such as “Do I like the coffee and how much?” or “Is this coffee of high quality?” Affective
assessment is any method that assesses acceptance, liking, preference, or emotions for a stimulus or stimuli.

但在咖啡杯测的情境中，这又意味着什么呢？我们可能使用描述性分析来客观评估咖啡的组
成风味，但我们如何衡量对咖啡风味的情感性反应呢？What does that mean in the context of a

CVA 使用一个术语来捕捉情感性评估在咖啡杯测中的本质：对质量的印象。「对质量的印

已知市场的偏好。CVA uses a term that captures the essence of affective assessment in a coffee cupping:
impression of quality. The impression of quality is a coffee taster’s opinion of the distinctiveness and desirability
of a coffee sensory category, reflecting either their own preference or a known market preference.
「您的偏好」是指您认为质量很高的咖啡。这是基于您身为咖啡品鉴师的经验与知识，以及

own experience and knowledge as a coffee taster, and your own tastes. The second category, “a known market
preference”, is based on understood norms and preferences in a particular segment of the coffee industry or
among a particular segment of consumers.
这就是杯测师从描述咖啡的感官属性转为表达对咖啡质量的印象，这个过程取决于杯测师在

主观性，它可能反映的是杯测师的个人反应，或者是已知市场的偏好（换句话说，就是预测
他人的情感性反应）。重要的是，我们不应期待在情感性衡量上，杯测师之间会有完全一致
的结果，因为它们是基于个人或市场的偏好，而这些偏好往往因人而异。So, this is how a
cupper switches from describing the coffee’s sensory attributes to expressing their impressions of a coffee’s
quality. This process relies on a cupper’s expert opinion, developed through experience. It’s important to remember
that this assessment, like all affective measures, is subjective. It might reflect a cupper’s personal response, or a
known market preference (in other words, predicting others’ affective response). Importantly, one would not
expect perfect alignment between cuppers on affective measurements, since they are based on individual or
market preferences, which are diverse.

评估表上的评论应该与偏好相关，而非仅仅列出描述性特征。Introduce learners to components of
the Affective Form, including the nine-point affective scale, uniformity, and defects. Clarify that comments on the
Affective Form should relate to preference and should not simply list descriptive characteristics.

Why Deliver an Interactive Online Course?
除了教授面对面的课程外，CVA 讲师也可以在实时（同步）在线环境中讲授CVA for

of the course, CVA trainers may deliver the CVA for Cuppers Course in a live (synchronous) online environment. This
appendix outlines how to adapt course content for this delivery method. Facilitating an online course allows trainers
to reach a wider audience and gain more flexibility in course scheduling.
在线授课注意事项
Important Considerations for Online Delivery
讲师需打造一个有助于学员达成课程目标的在线环境，讲师可以选择使用任何视频会议工具

live conversation, and learners should be able to see the trainer during the session. Trainers should minimize
distractions in the background and ensure that learners can hear them properly. The use of headphones or headsets
is recommended.
所有参与者必须具备稳定、高速的网络联机，并处于没有过多环境噪音的安静空间，所有参与

reliable, high-speed internet connection and a quiet space without excessive ambient noise. All participants must be
able to mute their microphones when necessary. Alternatively, trainers may use a platform that allows them to
mute and unmute participants’ microphones. Trainers may request that learners enable their webcams; this will
depend on the platform used, learner access to technology, and how the trainer wishes to conduct the course.
讲师应该在每次课程前、后各提供十五分钟的时间，协助学员排解技术问题并回答任何问题。
Trainers should be available for at least fifteen minutes before and after each session to greet learners as they
arrive, assist with technical troubleshooting, and address any questions.
在整个课程过程中，学员需要拍摄他们的作品（例如评估表）并将这些照片提交给讲师，以完

the trainer to complete the practical exam components. Photos can be submitted to trainers by email, messaging

题。讨论任何出现在学员评估表中的共同主题，并适时向小组提出反馈意见。Before introducing
the Combined Form, lead a reflective discussion on the homework. Ask learners to share their successes, challenges,
and questions. Discuss any common themes that emerged in the forms that were submitted and share feedback
with the group as appropriate.
考虑下列问题，并针对描述性评估进行反思：Consider the following questions for reflecting on the
descriptive assessment:
• 在进行第二次描述性评估后，您现在有什么感觉？How are you feeling now, after conducting a
second descriptive assessment?
• 您在第一次描述性评估之后，是否学到任何可在第二次评估中融会贯通的内容？Was there
anything you learned after the first descriptive assessment that you were able to integrate during the second
assessment?
考虑下列问题，并针对外在评估进行反思：Consider the following questions for reflecting on the extrinsic
assessment:
• 仅根据外在故事，哪一款咖啡是您的最爱？Based on the extrinsic stories alone, which coffee was
your favorite?
• 您最重视哪些外在属性？What extrinsic attributes do you value most?
• 如果另一款咖啡（不是最爱的那款）展现出您真正喜欢的感官属性，您会怎么办？这会让
您改变您最喜欢的咖啡吗？What if the other coffee (not the favorite) displayed sensory attributes that
you really enjoy? Would that change which coffee is your favorite?
