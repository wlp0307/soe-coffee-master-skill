咖啡Coffee # 1
想像美丽的比利时马利诺犬在山间自由奔跑，是多麽迷人的画面！咖啡庄园主人对牠
们充满热爱，牠们是真正的当地居民和农场伙伴，尤其在咖啡採收旺季，牠们与咖啡树和採摘工
人们和谐共处。首领狗查理勇敢地带领整个犬群，展现强大的领袖气势。How incredible is it to imagine
beautiful Belgian Malinois dogs roaming around in the mountains? The owner loves them immensely. They are true locals
and farm dogs, and you can see how comfortable they are around the coffee and the pickers during the peak season.
Charlie, the alpha dog, leads the rest of the pack fearlessly.
我们对这款咖啡同样充满期待～这款出色的El Roble 咖啡，来自秘鲁风景如画的卡哈玛卡地区，
儘管前往该地区的旅程充满挑战，但这款咖啡的卓越品质绝对值得造访。这款精緻的艺伎咖啡来
自阿尔伯特・爱因斯坦农场 — 阿尔伯特和他的家人细心採收每一颗咖啡樱桃，确保在最佳成熟时
期进行採摘。We are also excited about this coffee. This exceptional coffee, El Roble, is sourced from the picturesque
Cajamarca region of Peru. While the journey to this area is quite challenging, the coffee makes it worth it. This exquisite
Geisha coffee comes from the farm of Albert Einstein. Albert and his family meticulously harvested this coffee, ensuring
every cherry was picked at its peak ripeness.
农场座落于卡哈玛卡鬱鬱葱葱的山区，不仅种植咖啡，还有木薯、香蕉，偶尔还能看到羊驼，丰
富了当地的生物多样性并促进了永续发展。爱因斯坦对于品质的坚持，从种植到加工处理的每个
环节都一丝不苟，同时他对马利诺犬有着深厚的情感，这些狗总是陪伴在他身旁。Nestled in the lush
landscape of Cajamarca, the farm thrives not only with coffee but also with other crops such as cassava, banana, and the
occasional llama, contributing to the biodiversity and sustainability of the region. Einstein’s dedication to quality is evident
in every step of the coffee's journey, from cultivation to processing. Of course, he has an immense love for his Malinois
dogs that keep him company every step of the way.
El Roble 咖啡经过精细的处理工艺，採摘后的咖啡樱桃会在陶瓷槽中进行发酵，这种传统的方法
能增强咖啡独特的风味。发酵过程持续24小时，并严格控制，以确保达到最佳发酵状态。发酵完
成后，咖啡樱桃会经过清洗，最后在太阳能乾燥机中自然乾燥，利用太阳能温和地保存咖啡的完
整风味。The El Roble coffee undergoes meticulous processing. After being handpicked, the cherries are fermented in a
ceramic tank, a method that enhances the coffee's unique flavors. This is done traditionally over 24 hours, but with
meticulous control to ensure the coffee reaches the optimal fermentation point. Following fermentation and washing
through washing channels, the coffee is dried in a solar dryer, harnessing the natural energy of the sun to gently preserve
the beans' full potential.
当您品嚐El Roble 咖啡时，您品味的不仅是一杯咖啡，更是一段与善良的马利诺犬同行相伴的风
味之旅。这款咖啡的杯测分数高达89.8分，口感多汁且层次丰富，展现浓郁的果香与细緻的单宁
质地。您可以品嚐到木槿花、莓果糖、桃子、复盆子果酱及淡淡的黑胡椒香气，柔和如苹果酒般
的酸质，与饱满的口感完美结合，带来悠长绵密的焦糖馀韵，令人回味无穷。When you sip El Roble,
you're not just tasting a coffee; you're experiencing a journey of flavors and the company of kind dogs. With a cupping
score of 89.8, this coffee is remarkably juicy and complex, offering a symphony of intense fruit flavors and beautiful
tannins. You'll discover layered notes of hibiscus, berry candy, peach, raspberry jam, and subtle hints of black pepper. The
soft, cider-like acidity complements its full body, leading to a long, creamy, caramel finish that lingers delightfully on the
palate.
我们诚挚地邀请您品嚐这款由阿尔伯特和他心爱的马利诺犬用心打造的非凡咖啡，每一杯都是对
秘鲁优质咖啡的热情与坚持的最佳诠释。We invite you to savor this extraordinary coffee, crafted with passion
and care by Albert and his beloved Malinois dogs. Each cup is a testament to the rich flavors and dedication that define
the finest Peruvian coffees.

咖啡Coffee # 2
Saga 埃塞俄比亚日晒咖啡致力于展现强大的维京传统精髓，邀请您探索丰富的风味与围绕咖啡
的动人故事。Saga Ethiopia Natural has been crafted to embody the essence of the powerful Viking tradition. It is an
invitation to experience the intensity of flavors and incredible stories that surround coffee.
埃塞俄比亚被公认为阿拉比卡咖啡的发源地，因此这段咖啡旅程从这里展开再适合也不过。这款
埃塞俄比亚日晒咖啡来自衣索比亚谷吉产区着名的乌拉嘎处理厂，由维京咖啡精心挑选并烘焙。
这款卓越的咖啡採用 Wolisho 和 Kurume 品种，种植于海拔 2400m 的高地，高海拔环境与细
緻的日晒处理赋予这款咖啡独特的风味，使其成为精品咖啡界中的珍品。咖啡樱桃经手工採摘与
严格筛选，并在山区的非洲高架床上风乾20 多天。Ethiopia is commonly known as the birthplace of arabica
coffee, so it seems appropriate that this coffee’s journey begins there. The beans for this Ethiopia Natural originate from
the renowned Uraga washing station in Guji, Ethiopia. Procured and roasted by Viking Coffee, this exceptional blend
features the Wolisho and Kurume varieties, cultivated at a breathtaking altitude of 2400 meters above sea level. The high
elevation and meticulous natural process impart unique characteristics to the final brew, making it a true gem in the world
of specialty coffee. The cherries are picked, manually sorted, and dried carefully on African raised beds for 20+ days in the
mountain air.
当您准备冲煮 Saga 埃塞俄比亚日晒时，浓郁的草莓与香草香气随即瀰漫开来，预告着即将展开
的丰富体验。香气逐渐释放后，柑橘皮与成熟深色莓果的层次逐渐浮现，彷彿传统德国巧克力樱
桃蛋糕般诱人。每一口都能感受到成熟莓果与果酱般的甜美风味，交织出和谐悠长的馀韵，让人
回味无穷～As you prepare your cup of Saga Ethiopia Natural, the intense fragrance of strawberry and vanilla fills the
air, hinting at the rich experience to come. The aroma evolves, revealing layers of citrus peel and ripe dark berries—almost
like a traditional German chocolate and cherry cake. With each sip, the taste unfolds in a symphony of ripe berries and
jam-like sweetness, creating a harmonious balance that lingers on the palate.
沉浸在这款咖啡强烈又複杂的风味中，感受它向维京传统致敬的精神与纯粹黑咖啡的永恆魅力。
每一口，都将带您踏上一段非凡的风味旅程。Immerse yourself in the potent and complex flavors of a coffee
that pays homage to Viking tradition and the timeless allure of black coffee. Enjoy the powerful journey with every sip.

咖啡Coffee # 3:
如果您在咖啡界待得够久，您一定听过危地马拉，并且很可能知道安提瓜和薇薇特南
果，但或许您还未听过圣克里斯托巴尔、科班和阿尔塔维拉帕斯这些翠绿高地。 If you’ve been in
coffee long enough, you’ve heard about Guatemala. And you’ve most likely heard about Antigua and Huehuetenango.
However, maybe you haven’t heard of the lush highlands of San Cristobal, Cobán, Alta Verapaz.
这个地区以持续的降雨和云雾森林闻名，当您驾车上山前往农场时，细緻的雾气将四周环绕，让
一切看起来格外丰饶。就在这片雾气中，您会找到着名的 Finca Magica，这里的咖啡种植者
Aristoteles一生致力于栽培世界上最优质的咖啡，他的努力与奉献使他获得了「卓越杯国家获奖
者」的称号。在今年的收成中，Finca Magica 第三次获得卓越杯的荣誉，且值得一提的是，这
款咖啡曾被三位世界咖啡师大赛WBC 决赛选手以及两位世界冲煮大赛WBrC 决赛选手所使用，
这并不意外，但着实令人印象深刻。This area is known for its persistent rain and cloud forests. Even as you’re
driving uphill to the farms, you’re embraced by a fine mist that makes everything appear lush and rich. Here in these mists
lies the esteemed Finca Magica, where grower Aristoteles has dedicated his life to cultivating some of the finest coffees
in the world. His efforts and dedication helped him win the title of Cup of Excellence National Winner. In the current
harvest, Finca Magica won Cup of Excellence for the third time. It comes as no surprise (but is nonetheless impressive)
that this coffee has been used by three finalists in the World Barista Championship and two finalists of the World Brewers
Cup.
这座农场位于海拔1400-1500公尺的高地，土壤为黏土质，为Bourbon、Catuaí、Caturra和
Sarchimor 等咖啡品种提供了理想的生长环境。高海拔、湿润的气候与肥沃的土壤赋予了这款咖
独特的风味，使其既複杂又细緻。Finca Magica 也已经有超过八年的时间，持续拥有有机农场
认证。Nestled at an altitude of 1400 to 1500 meters above sea level, the farm thrives on clay loam soil, providing an
ideal environment for the coffee varieties Bourbon, Catuaí, Caturra, and Sarchimor. The combination of high altitude,
humidity, and rich soil imparts unique characteristics to the beans, resulting in a coffee that is both complex and refined.
Finca Magica has even been a certified organic farm for over eight years.
正如许多产区一样，採收季节通常充满挑战，Finca Magica 也不例外。他们採用了传统的全水
洗处理法，以保持咖啡的高品质。精心挑选採摘咖啡樱桃，并进行浮选（有时会进行多次），然
后去除果皮，再将这些咖啡豆放置到密封容器中进行发酵、清洗，最后放置在露天平台上，慢慢
地日晒乾燥，这些精心的处理过程使这款咖啡的风味更加丰富。Like in many places, harvest season is
intense and meticulous, and Finca Magica is no exception! A classic fully washed process is used to preserve the high quality
of the coffee. Cherries are carefully selected, floated (sometimes more than once), and then pulped. After that, they are
fermented in sealed tanks, washed, and finally placed on patios to be slowly sundried. All this enhances the flavors of this
extraordinary coffee.
这款来自危地马拉的精选咖啡，见证了种植者对品质的无比坚持。如果卓越杯和比赛评审都认为
它风味非凡，您觉得呢？ This celebration of Guatemalan craftsmanship is a testament to the grower’s dedication.
If Cup of Excellence and competition judges think it’s extraordinary—wouldn’t you agree?
