Section 1 – Measuring Water Composition
第一章－測量水的組成
● Time Available: 10 minutes for all three (3) parts
● Points Available: 15, 11 points required to pass
● Silent working is required.
● This section is designed to evaluate the Learner’s ability to:
○ Measure the Total Hardness and Alkalinity of a water sample
○ Record Total Hardness and Alkalinity measurements on the SCA Water Chart
○ Use an Electrical Conductivity meter to measure the estimated TDS of a water sample
● 測驗時間：總共10分鐘，分3個部分。
● 總分：15分，及格分數為11分。
● 作業中禁止交談。
● 本章節旨在評估學員下列能力：
○ 計算水質樣本的總硬度及鹼度
○ 將總硬度及鹼度的測量結果記錄在SCA水質圖上
○ 使用導電度計測量水質樣本的TDS概略值
