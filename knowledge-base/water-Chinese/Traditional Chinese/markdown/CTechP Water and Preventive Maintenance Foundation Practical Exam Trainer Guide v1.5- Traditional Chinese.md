This practical exam simulates tasks which are commonly performed during a preventive maintenance
visit. These tasks are divided into four (4) Sections. To receive full credit for each section the learner
must record all relevant observations, calculations, and task completions requested in the Practical
Exam Learner Worksheet.

學分，學員必須記錄下實務測驗學員工作表中所要求的所有相關觀察、計算和任務完成情況。

responsibility of the AST to ensure that:

All Learners are properly supervised at all times while working with hand tools
● 所有學員隨時都在適當的監督下操作手動工具。
An easily accessible, fully-equipped first aid kit is on site
● 現場備有完整配備且方便取用的急救箱。
All Learners use appropriate Personal Protective Equipment during course activities
● 所有學員在課程活動期間皆使用適當的個人防護配備。
All working stations are set up to meet the safe workplace recommendations detailed in
guidebook sections 1.01 and 1.06 and all local workplace safety standards
