工作坊说明与学习目标
Workshop Description and Learning Objectives
咖啡杯测入门工作坊旨在为非杯测者（无杯测经验的咖啡专业人士及消费者）提供工具，协助
他们分析并自述对于咖啡的感官感知。本工作坊专为初学者量身设计，提供友善的支持性环
境，引导学员探索咖啡评估的世界。本工作坊采理论与实务并行的方式，让学员透过参与品尝
与实作活动，有效学习评估咖啡感官属性的实用技巧。The Intro to Cupping Workshop provides non-
cuppers (coffee professionals without cupping experience, consumers) with tools to analyze and describe their
sensory perceptions of coffee. Specifically tailored to beginners, the workshop provides a welcoming and supportive
environment to explore the world of coffee evaluation. Participants will learn practical techniques to assess coffee’s
sensory attributes through engaging tastings and hands-on activities, bridging theory with practice.

感官评估很重要，因为它有助于我们发现咖啡的感官属性，属性是某些咖啡的特征，香气、
风味、质地......这些都是我们可以透过感官评估方法发现的感官属性。Sensory assessment is
important because it helps us discover coffees’ sensory attributes. Attributes are characteristics of a certain
coffee. Aromas, flavors, textures… all of these are sensory attributes that we can discover through sensory
assessment methods.
咖啡专业人士在许多情境下会使用感官评估做为决策工具，以决定在特定农场的何处种植咖
啡、选择适合种植的咖啡品种，以及使用何种加工处理方法、烘焙曲线以及冲煮方式。
Coffee professionals use sensory assessment as a decision-making tool in a variety of situations, including
where to plant coffee on a particular farm, which variety of coffee plant to grow, which processing method to
apply, which roast curve to use, and which brewing method to utilize.
杯测是一种对咖啡进行感官评估的方法，过程包括品尝多杯相同咖啡样本，每杯使用独立的
一组咖啡豆，分开研磨与冲煮。在今天的工作坊中，我们将介绍两种杯测方法。杯测可以用
来（1）描述咖啡的感官风味特性，或（2）挑选符合个人或特定目的偏好的咖啡。Cupping is
a method for the sensory assessment of coffee. It involves tasting several cups per coffee sample, using a
different set of coffee beans for each cup. These sets of beans are ground and brewed independently. We’ll cover
two approaches to cupping in today’s workshop. Cupping can be used to (1) describe the sensory profile of a
coffee or (2) identify preferred coffees for a particular individual or purpose.

品尝 #1
Tasting #1
学习目标：Learning Objectives:
• 定义并区别干香、湿香、风味以及余韵。Define and differentiate among fragrance, aroma, flavor,
and aftertaste.
• 辨别不同咖啡样本在感官风味特性上的差异。Detect differences in sensory profile among coffee
samples.
• 使用 CATA 列表记录咖啡样本的感官风味特性。Use CATA boxes to document the sensory
profile of coffee samples.
让我们先来探讨第一种杯测方法——描述性评估。在描述性杯测中，杯测者会识别并记录某
款咖啡的感官属性，以建立其风味轮廓。当我们采用这种描述性方法时，重点不在于我们是
否喜欢这款咖啡，也无关这款咖啡的「好」或「坏」，而是以客观且不带评论的方式，记录
我们感知到的感官属性。Let’s start by exploring the first cupping approach—a descriptive approach.
During a descriptive cupping, cuppers identify and document the variety of sensory attributes that comprise a
coffee’s sensory profile. When we use this descriptive approach, we’re not focusing on whether we like the coffee.
We’re not focusing on whether the coffee is “good” or “bad.” Rather, we’re objectively and without judgment
noting the sensory attributes we perceive.
有别于一般的咖啡饮用者，杯测者能够对感官感知进行细分并加以分析。想象一下，当白光
穿过棱镜时，它会被分解成单一颜色的光谱。同样地，在咖啡杯测中，我们会将整体的品饮
体验拆解为许多独立的风味元素。One skill that distinguishes cuppers from people who simply drink
coffee is their ability to break their sensory perceptions down into smaller parts and analyze them. Think about
the way in which white light is deconstructed into its single-color constituents when it goes through a prism—in a
similar way, we are deconstructing our holistic experience of a coffee into many distinct elements.
准备进行第一项品鉴活动前，让我们先探讨几个重要的感官特征：我们的嗅觉——也就是我
们感知气味的能力——极大地提升了我们对咖啡的感受（以及享受！）。
由于嗅觉在咖啡品尝中扮演如此重要的角色，咖啡专业人士会透过检视两个特征来分析咖啡
的气味：To prepare for our first activity, let’s explore a few characteristics we should examine when tasting
coffee. Our olfactory sense—our ability to smell—greatly enhances our perception (and enjoyment!) of coffee.
Because the olfactory sense plays such a powerful role in coffee tasting, coffee professionals analyze a coffee’s
smell by examining two characteristics:
• 第一个嗅觉相关特征是『干香』，干香是指咖啡粉在注水前所散发的气味。The first
olfactory-centered characteristic is called fragrance. Fragrance is the odor of coffee grounds before they
are brewed.
• 第二个特征是『湿香』，湿香是指当热水倒入咖啡粉后所产生的香气。The second
characteristic is called aroma. Aroma describes how the coffee smells once water has been added to the
coffee grounds.
我们在第一次品尝时还会探讨两个其他感官特征：The last two characteristics we’ll explore in our
first tasting are flavor and aftertaste.
我们对风味的感受来自两个部分：(1) 舌头感知的五种基本味觉——酸、咸、甜、苦、鲜—
—我们在品尝食物或饮料时所体验的主要味道；(2) 鼻后嗅觉：当咖啡液进入口腔后，鼻腔
内的气味感受。余韵指的是咖啡吞咽后残留于口腔中的感官体验。Our impression of flavor
comes from (1) the main tastes we experience when tasting foods or beverages—sour, salty, sweet, bitter,

我们已经讨论过这些我们即将评估的特征，接下来要讨论的是：我们该如何评估这些特征？
我们可以使用哪些词汇来描述它们？我们又该使用哪些工具来记录我们的印象？We’ve talked

words can we use to describe them? And what tools do we use to document our impressions?
我们接下来要使用的工具，就是「勾选所有适用的选项」（CATA）列表，咖啡专业人士会
使用 CATA 列表来有效记录他们发现的感官属性，CATA 列表的内容会因复杂程度而异，
我们今天将利用简化版协助大家认识 CATA 列表。We’ll use a tool called Check All That Apply
(CATA). Coffee professionals use lists of CATA boxes to efficiently document the sensory attributes they’re
detecting. CATA lists vary in their complexity. Today we’ll be using a simplified version to help you get started.
CATA 描述词来自感官词汇表与风味轮，这是 WCR、SCA 以及加州大学戴维斯分校共同
合作的成果。风味轮内圈可用于描述广泛且高层次的风味印象（例如：果香、坚果 / 可可、
烘烤）。若要描述更具体的风味印象，品鉴者可以使用风味轮来辨别每个主要分类下的子分
类，例如：莓果、柑橘类水果、果干）品鉴者也可以反向使用风味轮，根据具体的描述词来
对应适当的高层次主要风味类别。举例来说，如果品鉴者察觉到谷物风味，他们可以使用风
味轮来确定「烘烤」是最适当的高层次风味类别，用来描述这个感受。The CATA descriptors
come from the Sensory Lexicon and Flavor Wheel, the results of joint work by WCR, SCA, and the University of
California Davis. The inner circle of the Flavor Wheel can be used to describe broad, high-level impressions (like

响了我们对质量的印象。During our second cupping, we’ll use the nine-point scale to indicate our
impression of quality for a coffee overall. Overall describes our holistic perception of the coffee, taking all prior
cupping sections into account. Consider all the different sensory attributes and how well (or not) they
harmonize to meet the needs of a particular person or application. Although we’re still examining the same
sensory characteristics (fragrance, aroma, flavor, et cetera), this exercise is subjective because individual
preferences (and, therefore, impressions of quality) vary.
活动：杯测 #2（偏好性杯测）Activity: Cupping #2 (Cupping for Preference)
由讲师引导学员进行杯测，重点在于使用九分制情感性量表。讲师将在活动过程中，针对良
好杯测实务提供建议与反馈。本次杯测将包含两款咖啡样本，请参阅工作坊设备与用品清
单，查看建议的咖啡种类。This is a guided cupping that emphasizes the use of the nine-point affective
scale. The instructor provides reminders and feedback on good cupping practices during the activity. This
cupping includes two coffee samples. See the workshop Equipment and Supply List for suggested coffees.
1. 每款咖啡样本均准备五杯并置于托盘上，确保每桌至少有一组样本 A 以及一组样本
B 。Prepare a five-cup tray of each sample. Ensure that there is at least one tray of Sample A and one
tray of Sample B at each table.
2. 确保每位学员都有一份偏好性杯测工作表。Ensure that each learner has a copy of the Cupping
for Preference Worksheet.
3. 请学员反思自己对咖啡的偏好，并在工作表上写下自己喜欢的几个感官属性（例如：可
可风味、果香、高强度酸质）。Ask learners to reflect on their own coffee preferences and write
down a few sensory attributes they enjoy in coffee (e.g., cocoa notes, fruity notes, high-intensity acidity) on
their worksheet.
4. 接下来，请学员想象一位咖啡偏好与自己不同的咖啡饮用者。当每位学员确定了「另一
位咖啡饮用者」后，请学员在工作表上写下这位咖啡饮用者会喜欢的几个感官属性（例
如：烘烤风味、低强度酸质）。Next, ask learners to visualize a coffee drinker who has coffee
preferences that differ from their own. Once each learner has identified their “other coffee drinker,” ask
learners to write down a few sensory attributes that this coffee drinker enjoys (e.g., roasted notes, low-
intensity acidity) on their worksheet.

结论
Conclusion
进行反思练习及 / 或复习本次工作坊探讨的重点。向学员展示描述性及情感性评估表，并强调
本次工作坊使用的评估工具，正是目前咖啡专业人士进行咖啡感官评估所使用的工具。如果学

explore if they wish to continue developing their cupping skills.
