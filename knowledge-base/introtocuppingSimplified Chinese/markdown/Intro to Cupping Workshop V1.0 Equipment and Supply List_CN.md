咖啡
Coffee
品尝 #1：Tasting #1:
• 选择一款带有强烈果香调性的咖啡。One coffee should present strong fruity notes.
• 另一款咖啡则带有坚果、可可及 / 或烘烤调性。One coffee should present nutty, cocoa, and/or
roasted notes.
这两款咖啡的差异应能让初学者轻易辨识。例如：可选择阿拉比卡 vs. 罗布斯塔，或日晒（果
皮干燥）咖啡 vs. 水洗（羊皮层干燥）或湿剥法（种子干燥）的对比样品。The differences
between these two coffees should be easily noticeable to a beginner. Consider an arabica versus a robusta or a
natural (fruit-dried) coffee versus a washed (parchment-dried) or wet hulled (seed-dried) coffee.
品尝 #2：Tasting #2:
• 使用与品尝 #1 相同的两款咖啡，但将这两款咖啡的品尝顺序对调。Use the same two coffees
as in Tasting #1. Change the order in which the coffees are presented to learners.
杯测 #1：Cupping #1:
• 选择两款具有明显风味属性与强度差异的咖啡。Curate two coffees that present a variety of
descriptors and intensities.
进行杯测活动时，讲师可延用曾在工作坊品尝过的咖啡，前提是这两款咖啡之间的差异应能让初
学者轻易辨识。The instructor may repeat some of the coffees used earlier in the workshop as long as the
differences between the two coffees featured in the cupping are easily noticeable to a beginner.
杯测 #2：Cupping #2:
• 使用与杯测 #1 相同的两款咖啡，但将这两款咖啡的品尝顺序对调。Use the same two coffees
as in Cupping #1. Change the order in which the coffees are presented to learners.
场地
Facilities
工作坊的场地应该能让参与者在杯测桌边移动自如（每桌不超过六名参与者），并确保每位参
与者在工作坊听课时都有座位，上课场地应配备投影机与屏幕以便播放投影片，学员有桌面可
供书写及摆放咖啡样本等尤佳。The workshop space should allow participants to move around cupping
tables comfortably (with no more than six participants per table). Ensure that each participant has a seat for the
lecture portions of the workshop. The space should include a projector and screen for presenting slides. Preferably,
learners are seated at desks or something that provides a surface for writing, coffee samples, et cetera during the
presentation.
工作坊场地须具备足够电力，可同时支持滴滤式咖啡机、热水器以及磨豆机的使用，冲煮用水
应符合 CVA 标准中载明的规格。The workshop space should have the capacity to support the electrical
requirements of the batch brewer(s), hot water tower(s), and grinder(s) simultaneously. The water used for
brewing should meet the specifications outlined in the CVA Protocol.

Equipment and Cupping Supplies
讲师应根据参与人数调整所需的设备与用品数量。以下均为最低预估数量。为提升学员的学习
体验，讲师可以（1）增加托盘数量，确保每位学员同时都有托盘可供观察，并且（2）增加热
水壶数量，确保每位学员能够参与注水过程。The instructor should use the number of expected
participants to inform the quantity of equipment and supplies they procure. The estimates outlined below are
minimums. To improve the learners’ experience during the cuppings, the instructor may (1) increase the number of
trays so that each learner can be in front of a tray at a given time and (2) increase the number of kettles so that
each learner can actively participate in pouring.
