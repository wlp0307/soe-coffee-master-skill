第 1 大题 – 咖啡烘焙 Section 1 – Roasting
考试时间：150分钟 Time Available: 150 Minutes
总分：48分 Points Available: 48 points
• 每人 1 台烘焙机。
1 person per roaster.
• 本大题乃时间 x 温度实操测验，可使用任何品牌的烘焙记录仪软件进行记录。
This is a practical time x temp test, and it is to be logged using roast logger software of any
brand.
• 本大题旨在烘豆至指定颜色，并使烘焙曲线限制在较小的范围内，以展现您有能力控制烘
焙过程，达到各种极限。
The goal is to reach the target color and make the profile follow a path within a narrow limit to
show that you are able to control a roast to different extremes.
• 考试期间不得使用曲线记录软件中的参考曲线。
No reference profile can be used on the profile logging software throughout the examination
process.
• 根据以下规则，您于150分钟内最多可以进行 6 次烘焙。请准备一定量的咖啡生豆，以便
在规定时间内完成连续烘焙。
During the 150 minutes, you could end up roasting a maximum of 6 roasts according to the
rules below. Prepare an amount of green coffee so that you can complete back-to-back roasts
during the allotted time.
• 您可以任意读取咖啡生豆的资料，但限于规定时间内进行。
You may take any readings you wish of the green coffee and this must take place within the
allocated time.

1.1 烘焙 1 - 预热和确定最大 ROR
1.1 ROAST 1 - PREHEATING AND MAXIMUM-ROR DISCOVERY
1. 将烘焙机预热到讲师指定的温度。
Preheat the roaster to a temperature specified by the trainer.
2. 用全火（或讲师指定的最大火力）进行烘焙，直到烘焙至第一次爆裂和第二次爆裂间的中间点。
Perform a roast with full flame (or the max flame provided by the trainer) all the way to the midpoint
between 1st and 2nd crack.
3. 在烘焙记录仪软件上记录整个烘焙过程。烘焙过程中不得使用背景参考曲线。
Log the whole roasting process on the roast logger software. No background reference profile is allowed
during the roast.
4. 烘焙过程中的最大升温率（每30秒）为：______________。
The maximum rate of rise (per 30 seconds) during this roast is: ______________.
1.2 烘焙 2 - 参考色值制作（10 分）
1.2 ROAST 2 - REFERENCE COLOR CREATION (10 POINTS)
1. 将烘焙机预热到讲师指定的温度。
Preheat the roaster to a temperature specified by the trainer.
2. 根据烘焙 1 的结果，规划一个受控的烘焙曲线，通过烘焙过程调整燃烧器，以达到以下参考烘焙
指标：
Based on the result of Roast 1,plan a controlled roast profile where the burner is adjusted through the
roast cycle to achieve the following reference roast specification:
• 最终颜色介于 Agtron（或同等色度计）75–95 之间
An end color between Agtron (or equivalent colour meter) 75 – 95
• 第一次爆裂必须发生在以下范围内：8:30 分钟至 11:00 分钟
First crack must be in the range: 8:30 minutes to 11:00 minutes
• 发展时间必须在以下范围内：1:45 分钟至 2:45 分钟
Development time must be in the range: 1:45 minutes to 2:45 minutes
• 咖啡豆测温热电偶的上升率（ROR）必须始终高于每 30 秒 0.1°C/1°F。
Rate of rise for the bean temperature thermocouple must always be above 0.1°C/1°F per 30 seconds.
3. 使用烘焙记录仪软件打印烘焙记录，签字后交给 AST。
Print the roast from the roast logger software, sign it and hand it to the AST.
4. 从烘焙机中取出咖啡豆并置于室温时，AST将记录此次烘焙的咖啡粉色值（基于 3 个读数的平均
值）。这将成为烘焙 3 和 4 的参考色值。
When the beans are taken out of the roaster and are at room temperature, the AST will record the
ground color measurement of this roast (based on an average of 3 readings). This will become your
reference color for roasts 3 and 4.

第 4 大题 - 烘焙品质控制
SECTION 4 – ROAST QUALITY ASSURANCE
考试时间：20分钟 Time Available: 20 Minutes
总分：20分 Points Available: 20 points
• 考试应保持安静。Silent working is required.
• 必须在红光条件下进行测试。Test must take place under red light conditions.
• 本测试旨在评估您能否分辨烘焙曲线的大小差异，模拟生产质量控制流程。This test is
designed to evaluate your ability to distinguish between small and large differences in a roast
profile to simulate production quality control processes.
• 您必须对生产过程中的变化进行评估，并判断这些变化是否符合规范。You have to assess
variation in your production process and decide if the variations are IN or OUT of specification.
1. 您面前有 5 杯熟豆。其中一杯为参考组，供您与自行烘焙的咖啡豆进行比较。
In front of you are 5 coffees. One of them is the reference against which you will compare your
production roast.
2. 在4 杯熟豆中，每种烘焙都属于以下质量控制类别之一：
Amongst the 4 production roasts, each roast falls under one of the following quality control categories:
• 类别 1：符合标准
Category 1: On Standard
• 类别 2：最低标准（即：略有不同，但对于不熟悉咖啡的客户而言，差异并不明显，因此可
提供给大多数客群）
Category 2: Bottom Standard (i.e.:is slightly different but not noticeably different by an
uneducated customer so it can be released to most customer segments)
• 类别 3：略超出规范（即：对于熟悉咖啡的客户而言，与标准相差太大，但对于特定目标客
群而言，仍在其容许范围内而愿意接受）
Category 3: Just Out of Specification (i.e.:is too different from standard for an educated customer
but could be released depending on the variation tolerance threshold for the specific customer
segment targeted)
• 类别 4：不符合规范（即：差异太大，无法向任何客群提供）
Category 4: Out of Specification (i.e.:is too different to be released to any customer segment)
3. 每个样本的评分标准如下：
The scoring for each sample is as follows:
• 辨别瑕疵 = 1 分
Identify defect =1 point
• 说出瑕疵名称 = 1 分
Name roast defect = 1 point
• 分类结果 = 3 分
Category outcome =3 points
