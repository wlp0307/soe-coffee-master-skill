第 1 大题– 咖啡烘焙 SECTION 1 – ROASTING
考试时间：150 分钟 Time Available: 150 Minutes
总分：48 分 Points Available: 48 points
• 每人 1 台烘豆机。
1 person per roaster.
• 本大题乃时间 x 温度实操测验，可使用任何品牌的烘焙记录仪软件进行记录。
This is a practical time x temp test, and it is to be logged using roast logger software of any brand.
• 本大题旨在烘豆至指定颜色，并使烘焙曲线限制在较小的范围内，以展现学员有能力控制烘
焙过程，达到各种极限。
The goal is to reach the target color and make the profile follow a path within a narrow limit to
show that you are able to control a roast to different extremes.
• 考试期间不得使用曲线记录软件中的参考曲线。
No reference profile can be used on the profile logging software throughout the examination
process.
• 根据以下规则，学员于 150 分钟内最多可进行 6 次烘焙。请准备一定数量的咖啡生豆，以便
让学员于规定时间内完成连续烘焙。
During the 150 minutes, you could end up roasting a maximum of 6 roasts according to the rules
below. Prepare an amount of green coffee so that you can complete back-to-back roasts during
the allotted time.
• 学员可任意读取咖啡生豆的资料，但限于规定时间内进行。
You may take any readings you wish of the green coffee and this must take place within the
allocated time.

describe the characteristics of the defect.

You will be presented with 6 sets of 3 cups coded in triangulation test format.

然后Then
3. 确定异常样本是目标豆还是瑕疵豆（1分）。

● 考试应保持安静。Silent working is required.
● 必须在红光条件下进行测试。Test must take place under red light conditions.
● 本测试旨在评估学员能否分辨烘焙曲线的大小差异，模拟生产质量控制流程。This test is
designed to evaluate your ability to distinguish between small and large differences in a roast
profile to simulate production quality control processes.
● 学员必须对生产过程中的变化进行评估，并判断这些变化是否符合规范。You have to assess
variation in your production process and decide if the variations are IN or OUT of specification.
1. 学员面前有 5 杯熟豆。其中一杯为参考组，供您与自行烘焙的咖啡豆进行比较。
In front of you are 5 coffees. One of them is the reference against which you will compare your
production roast.
2. 在4 杯熟豆中，每种烘焙都属于以下质量控制类别之一：
Amongst the 4 production roasts, each roast falls under one of the following quality control categories:
• 类别 1：符合标准
Category 1: On Standard
• 类别 2：最低标准（即：略有不同，但对于不熟悉咖啡的客户而言，差异并不明显，因此可
提供给大多数客群）
Category 2: Bottom Standard (i.e.:is slightly different but not noticeably different by an
uneducated customer so it can be released to most customer segments)
• 类别 3：略超出规范（即：对于熟悉咖啡的客户而言，与标准相差太大，但对于特定目标客
群而言，仍在其容许范围内而愿意接受）
Category 3: Just Out of Specification (i.e.:is too different from standard for an educated customer
but could be released depending on the variation tolerance threshold for the specific customer
segment targeted)
• 类别 4：不符合规范（即：差异太大，无法向任何客群提供）
Category 4: Out of Specification (i.e.:is too different to be released to any customer segment)
