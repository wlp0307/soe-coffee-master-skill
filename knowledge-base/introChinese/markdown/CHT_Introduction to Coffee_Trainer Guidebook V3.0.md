2. 課程說明與更新
Course Description and Updates
咖啡導論課程為新進咖啡專業人士與消費者提供精品咖啡價值鏈的概論，並介紹歷史、種植、
處理、烘焙、感官評估以及沖煮準備的核心概念。學員將透過多樣化的品鑑練習，深入了解價
值鏈中各項決策如何影響最終的咖啡風味。Introduction to Coffee provides new coffee professionals and
consumers with a general overview of the specialty coffee value chain and introduces key concepts in history,
cultivation, processing, roasting, sensory assessment, and preparation. Participants will engage in a variety of
tasting exercises to deepen their understanding of how decisions made along the value chain impact the final cup.

SCA 建議參加完全線上課程的學員，使用批量滴濾式咖啡機、手沖設備或單次份量產品（例
如浸泡式咖啡）來準備四個品鑑活動。The SCA recommends that learners participating in a fully online
course use a batch brewer, a manual device, or a set of individually portioned products (e.g., coffee that can be
steeped) to prepare the four tasting activities.
當採用線上方式進行授課時，講師應：When conducting an online course, trainers should:
• 在學員註冊課程後，儘早通知學員所需設備清單，講師可選擇提供設備或請學員自行準
備。Communicate the list of required equipment to learners as soon as they register for the course. Trainers
may provide equipment or ask learners to procure equipment on their own.
• 為確保一致性，訓練師可以向學員寄送他們在每個活動中將使用的咖啡樣品。（在學員註
冊課程時詢問他們的郵寄地址，並及時寄送物資箱，確保學員在課程開始前收到。）To
ensure consistency, trainers may send the learners samples of the coffees they will use in each activity. (Ask for
the learner’s mailing address when they register for the course and mail the supply box in time for learners to
receive the box before the course.)
o 樣品應事先研磨以方便學員使用，並確保一致性達到最佳效果。Samples should be
pre-ground for the learner’s convenience and to maximize consistency.
o 樣品應分別包裝在有獨立編碼且密封的袋中，每個包裝分別對應四個活動。建議提
供足量樣品，以便學員在必要時能重複進行活動（以防出錯）。Samples should be
packaged in individually coded, sealed bags for each of the four activities. Consider providing enough
of each sample so the learner can do the activity twice (in case they make a mistake).

建議，以確保學員選擇的咖啡具備每個活動所需的適當屬性。查看活動描述以獲取學員需

ensure that the coffees learners source contain the appropriate attributes for each activity. Review the activity
descriptions for details on the types of coffees learners need to source.

demonstrate how to prepare the samples. Provide clear guidance on the quantities of coffee and water, the
water temperature, and the brew time to use.
• 每次活動結束後，請給予學員足夠的時間清潔設備並準備材料，以進行下一個活動。Once
an activity is complete, allow time for learners to clean and reset their supplies before the next activity.
