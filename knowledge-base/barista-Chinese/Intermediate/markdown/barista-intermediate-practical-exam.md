You will Indicate your assessment of extraction level on the scale supplied on the form

EXTRACTION VERIFICATION 萃取率确认
Your examiner will check that you are within 1 point on the scale from your examiner’s
independent assessment. If not, they will explain the reason(s) and this will not be timed.
考官会审核考生答案是否与考官答案差值在1分之内，若否，考官会向考生解释原因，解释
部分不计时。
The examiner may choose to physically measure the extraction percentage to support their
assessment. 若需要，考官可选择使用相关测量工具测出萃取率百分比以支持其结果。
For this section, balanced extraction is deemed to be between 18-22%. This measurement
will not be timed. 该部分，平衡萃取萃取率需在18-22%之间。测量部分不计时。
AROMA, FLAVOR, BODY VERIFICATION 香气、风味、体脂感审核
Your examiner will check that your descriptors are reasonably accurate. If not, they will
explain the reason(s) and this will not be timed. 考官将审核描述是否一定程度上准确合理，
若否，考官会向考生解释原因，解释部分不计时。

2. DRINK PRODUCTION ORDER (8 MINUTES) 饮品出品（8分钟）
You will produce the set of drinks listed on the table below. 考生要求出品以下表格所列饮品。
If you go over time, you will incur an automatic fail on this section. 若超时，则该部分考试将
自动判定为不合格。
Correct routine should be observed throughout. Any missed step will incur a 1 point
deduction for each drink affected. 考官将全程观察考生操作是否遵循咖啡师出品常规操作步
骤标准，若有遗漏则相应扣分，一个饮品的一个步骤扣1分。
All drinks should be presented to the following standards 所有饮品须符合以下标准:
Beverages are based on a single shot of espresso. 考生须以单份浓缩为基底进行出品。
Milk drinks should be made in 150 - 240ml (5-8oz) cups or glasses. 牛奶饮品须使用大小介于
150-240毫升（5-8盎司）之间的杯子或玻璃杯。
Cappuccinos should have a foam depth between 5-15mm in the cup. Caffe Lattes should
have a foam depth between 3-10mm in the cup and have less foam depth than
Cappuccinos. 卡布奇诺奶沫深度须在5-15毫升之间，拿铁奶沫深度须比卡布奇诺浅，在3-
10毫升之间。
All presented beverages should be full to =/< 5mm from the edge of the cup. 所有饮品奶沫最
上层距离杯缘须=/<5毫米。
All milk based beverages should have a temperature between 50- 65̊C (122-150F̊ ) in the
cup. 所有包含牛奶的饮品温度须在50-65度（122-150华摄氏度）之间。
Your assessor will advise on local presentation standards (cup type, saucers etc) but
these are not tested. 考官会告知考生符合当地标准的饮品呈递方式（杯子风格，盘子等），
但此内容不计入考试范围。
The Cappuccino and Caffe Lattes made during the production order do not need to display
Latte Art, although you may choose to present them this way. 卡布奇诺和拿铁在制作中不要
求有拉花图案，但可以有。
You will undertake the Latte Art task separately in Section 3. 拉花属于实操考试第3部分考试
内容。
You are required to work in a clean manner. Your assessor may request that you clean
up your workspace. This will be done within your assessment time. 考生在制作过程中

3. LATTE ART (5 MINUTES) 拉花（5分钟）
You will produce the pair of drinks listed below. 请考生出品以下所列要求出品的两杯饮品。
You can discard as many drinks as necessary, but you must present 2 drinks for assessment at
the end of the period (or before). 在时间允许范围内考生可重复制作多次，但必须在考试时间结
束之前将最满意的2杯饮品呈递给考官进行最后评估。
If you go over time, you will incur an automatic fail on this section. 若超时，则该部分考试将自
