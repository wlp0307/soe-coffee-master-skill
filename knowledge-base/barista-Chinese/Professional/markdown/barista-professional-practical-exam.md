4.1 浓缩咖啡基底和工作程序ESPRESSO BASE & WORKING ROUTINE
• 您应使用单份意式浓缩咖啡作为每种饮料的基础，并以相同的150-240ml（5-8oz）杯子
或玻璃杯装。You should use a single shot of espresso as the base for each drink and make each in identical 150 -
240ml (5-8oz) cups or glasses.
