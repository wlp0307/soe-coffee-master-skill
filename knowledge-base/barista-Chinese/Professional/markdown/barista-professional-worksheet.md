1. 水化学评估
WATER CHEMISTRY EVALUATION
目的
Objective:
• 评估水化学（碱度和总硬度）对冲煮咖啡感官品质的影响 To evaluate the effects of water
chemistry (alkalinity and total hardness) on the sensory qualities of brewed coffee.
时间：30分钟
Time: 30 minutes
设备：
Equipment:
• SCA杯测协议中描述的设备可以在这里找到：https://sca.coffee/research/protocols-
best-practices The equipment described in the SCA Cupping Protocol here:
• 另外，还有3个手动水壶和一个精确的温度计
Plus, 3 manual kettles and an accurate thermometer
说明: Instructions:
• 从AST处分别获得3种不同类型的水每种750毫升（非常硬，理想的SCA，非常软/
蒸馏水)。
Obtain from the AST 750mls each of 3 different water types (very hard, SCA ideal, very soft/distilled).
• 每种水保留约20毫升进行测试。
Retain approximately 20mls of each water for testing.
• 以固定的研磨大小并一次连续地为9个杯测碗研磨足够的咖啡。
Grind up adequate coffee for 9
cupping bowls at a fixed grind size and in a single, continuous batch.
• 每种水使用1 x水壶，每种水冲泡3杯相同的咖啡，并注意在恒定温度下输送每种
水。
Using 1 x kettle per water type, brew 3 identical bowls of coffee, per water type, taking care to deliver each water
at a consistent temperature.
• 品尝前，从每套至少1碗中取出20毫升咖啡，并留作测试。
Before tasting, withdraw 20mls of coffee
from at least 1 bowl per set and retain for testing.
• 测试保留的水样品的临时和永久硬度，并使用ppm CaCO3当量标度在SCAE水表
上作图（您的审查员将对此进行解释）。
Test the retained water samples for Temporary and
Permanent Hardness and plot on the SCAE Water Chart, using a ppm CaCO3 equivalent scale (your examiner will
explain this).
• 测试保留的咖啡的TDS％和提取％。
Test the retained coffee for TDS % and Extraction %.
• 填写下面的感官和测量属性表。
Complete the Sensory & Measured Attributes table below.
• 填写下表后，请完成“结论”部分。
Complete the Conclusion section after you fill in the table below.

2. 填压和布粉评估
TAMPING & DISTRIBUTION EVALUATION
目的：
Objective:
• 评估不同布粉方法对冲煮的意式浓缩咖啡的影响
Evaluate the effects of different distribution
methods on brewed espresso.
• 评估不同的压粉方法对冲煮的意式浓缩咖啡的影响。 Evaluate the effects of different
tamping methods on brewed espresso.
时间：60分钟
Time: 60 minutes
设备：
Equipment:
• 1个市售布粉工具（例如OCD） 1 x commercially available distribution tool (e.g. OCD)
• 1 x正确安装的平面型压粉器（通常为58.4mm直径） 1 x correctly fitting flat based tamper
(usually 58.4mm diameter)
• 1 x不正确安装的平面型压粉器（通常直径为57-58mm） 1 x incorrectly fitting flat based
tamper (usually 57-58mm diameter)
• 1套范围超过40kgs / 90lbs的磅秤（通常是浴室磅秤）标准的意式浓缩咖啡生产设
置
1 x set of scales with a range exceeding 40kgs/90lbs (usually bathroom scales) A standard espresso production
setup
• 标准的意式浓缩咖啡测量设置（折光仪和相关工具） A standard espresso measuring setup
(refractometer and associated tools)
• 无底手柄portafilter（可选，但对于视觉反馈很有用） Bottomless portafilter (optional, but useful
for visual feedback)
说明： Instructions:
• 使用任何特定的压粉工具或布粉流程，但流程必须是可重复的并且将成为您的参
照冲煮。
Use any particular tamping tool or distribution routine, but routine must be repeatable and will be your
control brew.
• 使用天平或任何其他准确的方法来找到一致的填压压力。
Use scales or any other accurate
method to find a consistent tamp pressure.
• 对AST校准的配方/方法制作3杯双份浓缩咖啡。
Make 3 double espressos to the recipe/method
calibrated by the AST.
• 测量每种冲泡的实际剂量/产量，并计算出准确的EBF /比率。
Measure actual dose/yield
for each brew and calculate accurate EBF/Ratios.
• 测试并品尝每种冲煮
Test and taste each brew.
• 计算每个参数的平均平均值和标准偏差（SD）（您的培训师会帮助您解决，并解
释为什么这些是有用的数据点）。
Calculate mean averages and standard deviation (SD) for each
parameter (your trainer will help with this and explain why these are useful data points).
• 将结果记录在下面“结果”部分的表1：对照配方/方法中。
Record the results in Table 1: Control
Recipe/Method in the Results section below.
